Privacy Policy — Sorry Babushka

Last updated 15 August 2026

Sorry Babushka is a language-learning app published by Mitch Goulson. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information. The short version: Sorry Babushka does not collect any data about you.

What we collect

Nothing. Sorry Babushka has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party trackers. The app makes no network requests. We operate no server, and no information about you or your use of the app is ever transmitted to us or to anyone else.

What is stored on your device

The app saves your settings and progress locally on your device so it can pick up where you left off: the language you chose, the name you gave your grandmother, your intensity level, which scenes you have played, which phrases you have learned, and how many sentences you have said out loud. This never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes all of it. You can also erase it from inside the app at any time under Settings → Start over from the beginning.

Microphone and speech recognition

Sorry Babushka teaches pronunciation, so it needs to hear you say the phrase you are learning. When you tap the microphone button, and only then, the app records what you say and converts it to text using Apple's own speech recognition built into iOS. It compares that text with the phrase you were practising, shows you which words came out wrong, and then discards it.

We never store your audio. We never store the transcript. Neither is transmitted to us, because the app has no server to transmit it to. Depending on your device and settings, Apple's speech recognition may process audio on Apple's servers; that processing is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not ours. The app also works without the microphone — it will simply take your word for it that you said the phrase.

Children

Sorry Babushka teaches adult language, including profanity, and is not directed at children. The app asks your age on first launch and limits the content it will teach you accordingly. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone of any age, because we do not collect information.

Changes

If a future version of the app starts collecting anything, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described in the app's release notes.
